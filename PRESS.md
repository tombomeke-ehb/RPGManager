# RPG Manager — Press Kit

**One-liner**
A console RPG where your heroes share a living world — what one does changes everything for the others.

**Short description**
RPG Manager is a text-based RPG built with .NET 8 and C#. You play as the Keeper of Destiny, managing a roster of heroes in the realm of Tavaryn. Each hero has their own class and story — but they all exist in the same living world. Actions one character takes ripple across all the others: NPCs remember what your heroes have done, new paths unlock, and reputations shift. No single class can handle everything; building the right roster is the game.

**The core mechanic**
A shared world state connects all characters. When your Warrior clears a bandit camp, merchants in that region treat your Mage better too. When your Mage breaks a magical seal, your Archer can now walk through the door it was blocking. Quests completed, enemies defeated, NPCs helped or harmed — every action is remembered by the world, not just the character who did it.

**Key features**
- Three hero classes: Warrior, Mage, and Archer — each with unique strengths and access to different content
- Shared world state: actions from any character affect NPCs, prices, dialogue, and world flags for all others
- Character roster with milestone-based unlocks (new slots open as characters level up)
- Turn-based combat with elemental damage and status effects *(in development)*
- NPC dialogue trees that react to reputation, completed quests, and active character class *(in development)*
- Class-exclusive quest content — some paths only open for specific classes *(in development)*
- Regional reputation system — how each region of Tavaryn views the Keeper *(in development)*
- JSON save system, self-contained builds, cross-platform (Windows, macOS, Linux)
- The Hollow Court: a secret society operating in the shadows across all of Tavaryn, the long-arc antagonist *(in development)*

**Current status**
Actively in development. The core engine is complete: character creation, equipment, inventory management, and the save system are all working. Combat, world travel, the quest system, and the Ashlands (the first playable region) are the current development focus. See ROADMAP.md for milestone targets.
